Questions

How big a thing can the claw pick up?

Small, light things, like a phone. For anything heavy we find it and mark it so it can be lifted another way.

Can you cover a large area?

We work on a tether from shore or a boat, so we cover an area in passes. Tell us the size of the area and we will plan it with you.

Do you work outside Halifax?

Yes. We work across Atlantic Canada, and further for larger jobs.
