Services

Wharf, mooring and dam inspections

Video of the parts of your structure that sit underwater, so your maintenance crew or engineer can plan from it before anyone gets in the water.

What we look at

  • Pilings and wharf faces
  • Mooring chain, shackles and blocks
  • Dam faces, intakes and trash racks
  • Outfalls, pipes and outlet works
  • Debris and scour around footings

When people call us

  • Before the season, to check moorings
  • Planning wharf or piling repairs
  • Checking an intake, screen or outlet
  • After a storm or ice

What you get

  • The full recorded video from the dive
  • An edited clip of the parts that matter
  • Still images
  • A short written summary of what we saw (see a sample)

Questions

How deep can you go?

The ROV is rated to 150 m (500 ft) on a 300 m tether, which covers almost every wharf, mooring and dam in the region.

Do you measure wear or thickness?

No. We give you clear video and stills of what's there. If you need measurements, your engineer or mooring service can use our footage to decide where to look.
