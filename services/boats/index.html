Services

Hull, propeller and thruster inspections

We put the ROV under your boat while it stays in the water, so you can see the hull, running gear and thrusters without hauling out or sending a diver down.

What we look at

  • Hulls and bulbous bows
  • Propellers, shafts and rudders
  • Bow and stern thruster tunnels
  • Sea chests, intakes and zincs
  • Damage after a grounding or a strike

When people call us

  • Before buying or selling a boat
  • After a grounding, or if you think you hit something
  • Before a long trip, or after a winter in the water
  • When your surveyor or insurer wants video

What you get

  • The full recorded video from the dive
  • An edited clip of the parts that matter
  • Still images
  • A short written summary of what we saw (see a sample)

Questions

Does the boat need to come out of the water?

No. The ROV goes under the boat where it sits, at the wharf, on a mooring or at anchor.

Can you work from our boat?

Yes. We can launch from a wharf, a dock, or your own vessel.

Will you tell us if something needs fixing?

We describe what the footage shows and point out anything that stands out. Whether it needs repair is up to your surveyor or engineer.
