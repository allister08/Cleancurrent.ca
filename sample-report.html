1Scope

Film the mooring riser chain from mid-water down to the block, and the block where the chain meets it, so the owner can see its condition without hauling it.

2What we saw

  1. Marine growth covers most of the riser chain we filmed, from mid-water down to near the bottom. Stills A and C
  2. The lowest links, just above the block, are mostly clear of growth and show orange surface rust. Stills B and D
  3. The chain reaches the concrete block and the block is sitting on the bottom, with growth on its top surface. Stills B and D

3Stills from the footage

4For your surveyor or mooring service

  • This is a visual record only. We didn't measure link thickness or wear, and we didn't clean any growth off the chain.
  • The rusted links at the block, and any links hidden under growth, are worth checking up close at the next service.
  • We describe what's in the footage. Whether anything needs repair or replacing is for your surveyor or mooring service to decide.
