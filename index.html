Who we are

Clean Current is Allister Roy and Brent Steeves. We started in January 2026 with a SeaBotix LBV150 and rebuilt it with current Blue Robotics electronics. We call it Frank.

Allister flies the ROV and edits the footage. Brent handles quotes, planning and paperwork. Whoever answers your email is on site for the job.

Most jobs take a few hours. We set up on the dock, the wharf or your boat, you watch the live feed with us if you want to, and you get the video afterwards.

How a job works

  1. 1

    Tell us what and where

    The boat or structure, where it is, roughly how deep, and when you need it done.

  2. 2

    We agree the plan

    What we'll film, access, any permissions, and the weather window.

  3. 3

    We dive

    From the wharf, the dock or your boat. You can watch the live feed with us.

  4. 4

    You get the footage

    The video, stills and a short summary of what we saw. See a sample report

What we can film

We film it and tell you what we saw. Whether something needs fixing is up to your engineer or surveyor.

Boats and ships

A look under the hull before or after a trip, a grounding, or if you think you hit something.

  • Hulls and bulbous bows
  • Propellers and rudders
  • Thruster tunnels and sea chests

Wharves, moorings and dams

Video your maintenance crew or engineer can plan from before anyone gets in the water.

  • Pilings and wharf faces
  • Mooring chain and blocks
  • Dam faces, intakes and trash racks
  • Outfalls and outlet works

Surveys and cleanups

The ROV and a pilot working for your team, wherever you need eyes on the bottom.

  • Finding lost gear and debris
  • Seabed and habitat video
  • Light recovery with the claw

Filming and expeditions

4K underwater footage for wreck crews, researchers, and documentary or social media teams.

  • Shipwrecks and dive sites
  • Marine life and habitat
  • A second camera or ROV pilot for your team

We're also working on propeller cleaning with the ROV. If you'd like to try it early, let us know.
