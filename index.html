01 / Services

Focused work below the waterline.

01

ROV visual inspections

Underwater video of hulls, propellers and other accessible vessel components. Review visible conditions before maintenance or document completed work.

↗ 02

Marine infrastructure

Visual inspections of accessible docks, pilings, moorings, intakes and outfalls. Recorded footage and still images help your team plan maintenance and follow-up work.

↗ 03

Environmental survey support

ROV and operator support for seabed video, habitat transects and repeat monitoring. We collect footage under your survey team’s direction for your specialists to interpret.

↗ 04

Propeller cleaning development

We’re developing a robotic approach to underwater propeller cleaning and polishing. Contact us about development partnerships and future trials.

↗

Our surface-powered SeaBotix ROV uses Blue Robotics electronics and carries a camera and claw manipulator, with approximately 300 metres of tether. Each job is scoped around site conditions, access and the required survey method. Visual footage supports assessment by your engineer, surveyor or environmental specialist.
