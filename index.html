Underwater video of your hull, mooring or wharf.

We're Allister and Brent. We run an underwater ROV out of Halifax and Dartmouth, film what's down there, and send you the footage with a short write-up.

Ask about a job See our work

No divers in the water

Our ROV goes down on a tether while we watch the live camera on the wharf. You see the bottom of your boat, your mooring or your wharf the same day, without hauling anything out or putting anyone in the water.

It's just the two of us, so the person you email is the person on site for the job.

More about us and the ROV

What we do

We film it and tell you what we saw. Whether something needs fixing is up to your engineer or surveyor.

Boats and shipsA look under the hull without hauling out: hull, propellers, rudders and thrusters. Wharves, moorings and damsPilings, mooring chain and blocks, dam faces, intakes and outfalls. Surveys and cleanupsFinding lost gear, filming the seabed, and light recovery with the claw. Filming and expeditions4K footage for wreck crews, researchers and documentary teams.
