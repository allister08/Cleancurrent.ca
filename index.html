Underwater inspections in Halifax.

We send our ROV down to film your hull, propeller, mooring or the seabed, then give you the video and a short report on what we found.

Request an inspection

How a job works

Most inspections are a few hours on site. You deal directly with the two people who run the ROV.

  1. 1

    Tell us what and where

    The vessel or structure, its location, roughly how deep, and when you need it done.

  2. 2

    We confirm the plan

    Scope, access, permissions and conditions, agreed with you before we arrive.

  3. 3

    We dive

    From the wharf, the dock or your vessel. You can watch the live feed as we go.

  4. 4

    You get the footage

    Recorded video, still images and a short written summary of what we saw.

What we inspect

We film it and point out what we see. Your engineer or surveyor decides what needs doing.

Vessels

A look under the hull before or after a voyage, a grounding, or a suspected strike.

  • Hulls and bulbous bows
  • Propellers and rudders
  • Thruster tunnels and sea chests

Wharves and moorings

Video your maintenance crew can plan from before anyone gets wet.

  • Pilings and wharf faces
  • Mooring chain and blocks
  • Intakes and outfalls

Survey and cleanup support

An ROV and operator working under your team's direction.

  • Lost gear and debris location
  • Seabed and habitat video
  • Light recovery with the claw

We're also developing ROV-based propeller cleaning. If you'd like to be part of early trials, get in touch.
